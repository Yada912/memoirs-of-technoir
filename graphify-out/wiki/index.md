# Memoirs of Technoir - Knowledge Graph Wiki

Welcome to the internal engineering wiki for **Memoirs of Technoir**.

## Core Subsystems
- **[Game Lifecycle & State Management](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/singletons/GameManager.gd)**: Manages transitions between Start, Home, ChapterSelect, StoryReader, and Workstation3D.
- **[Audio Synthesis Engine](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/audio/ProceduralAudio.gd)**: Procedural 16-bit PCM WAV synthesizer generating all mechanical sound effects in-memory.
- **[Data-Driven Chapter Schema](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/data/ChapterData.gd)**: Custom resource architecture allowing rapid creation of new narrative chapters.
- **[3D Machine Repair Framework](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/core/RepairableObject.gd)**: Dependency resolution graph, physical disassembly constraints, and operational test run validation.
- **[Procedural 3D Fan Generator](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/procedural/ProceduralFan.gd)**: Zero-asset procedural 3D model with interactive parts and live motor rotation.
- **[Workstation Controller](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/controllers/WorkstationController.gd)**: Multi-touch pinch-zoom, turntable orbit, raycasting, and tray sorting.
- **[Figma Design System](file:///d:/work/memoirs-of-technoir/scripts/ui/UIStyleHelper.gd)**: Tokens, color palettes, and styles matching the Figma reference.
